# 🎨 Dashboard Design

## Objective

The dashboard is designed to present complex Google Play Store data in a clear, interactive, and business-focused format. The design prioritises usability, executive reporting, and data-driven decision-making by transforming analytical results into meaningful visual insights.

---

# Design Principles

The dashboard follows the following design principles:

- Simplicity and clarity
- Consistent visual formatting
- Business-focused storytelling
- Interactive exploration
- Executive-friendly layout
- Minimal visual clutter

---

# Dashboard Layout

The dashboard is organised into logical analytical sections.

## 1. Executive KPI Section

Displays high-level marketplace performance using KPI cards.

### Key Metrics

- Total Applications
- Total Categories
- Total Installs
- Total Reviews
- Average Rating
- Average Price

**Business Value**

Provides executives with an immediate overview of marketplace performance.

---

## 2. Product Performance

Visualises application performance across categories.

### Example Visuals

- Top Categories
- Top Installed Applications
- Highest Rated Applications

**Business Value**

Helps identify successful products and high-performing market segments.

---

## 3. Customer Engagement

Measures user interaction using ratings and reviews.

### Example Visuals

- Ratings Distribution
- Review Volume
- Customer Satisfaction by Category

**Business Value**

Evaluates customer engagement and user satisfaction.

---

## 4. User Adoption

Measures marketplace reach using installation data.

### Example Visuals

- Installs by Category
- Top Installed Applications
- Category Market Share

**Business Value**

Highlights product popularity and market penetration.

---

## 5. Pricing Analysis

Evaluates monetisation strategies.

### Example Visuals

- Free vs Paid Applications
- Average Price by Category
- Paid Application Distribution

**Business Value**

Supports pricing and monetisation decisions.

---

## 6. Marketplace Intelligence

Provides strategic insights into market structure.

### Example Visuals

- Category Distribution
- Content Rating Analysis
- Android Version Distribution

**Business Value**

Supports product planning and market opportunity analysis.

---

# Interactive Features

The dashboard includes interactive capabilities such as:

- Category filters
- Content Rating filters
- Application Type filters
- Drill-down analysis
- Cross-filtering between visuals

---

# Design Outcome

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor business performance, explore marketplace trends, evaluate product success, and make informed strategic decisions through an intuitive and interactive reporting experience.
