# Dashboard Overview

## Purpose

The dashboard provides an executive view of Google Play Store performance by consolidating key business metrics into an interactive reporting solution.

The dashboard enables users to explore marketplace trends, product performance, customer engagement, and pricing strategies through dynamic visualisations.

---

# Target Users

- Product Managers
- Business Analysts
- Data Analysts
- Growth Teams
- Marketing Teams
- Executive Leadership

---

# Dashboard Pages

## Executive Summary

Provides a high-level overview of marketplace performance using KPIs and summary charts.

---

## Product Performance

Analyses application performance based on installs, ratings, reviews, and category.

---

## Customer Engagement

Measures customer interaction using ratings and review metrics.

---

## Pricing Analysis

Compares Free and Paid applications to evaluate monetisation strategies.

---

## Marketplace Intelligence

Identifies market trends, competitive categories, and growth opportunities.

---

# Expected Outcome

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor performance, identify opportunities, and support evidence-based product decisions.
