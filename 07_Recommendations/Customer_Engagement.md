# 👥 Customer Engagement

## Objective

Improve customer satisfaction and strengthen long-term user engagement.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

- Encourage users to provide ratings and reviews.
- Respond proactively to customer feedback.
- Reduce issues affecting application ratings.
- Enhance application usability and performance.
- Monitor customer sentiment to identify recurring concerns.

---

## Expected Business Impact

- Higher customer satisfaction
- Increased engagement
- Improved application ratings
- Stronger customer loyalty
