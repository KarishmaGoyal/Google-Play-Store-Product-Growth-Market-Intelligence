# 📱 Product Strategy

## Objective

Recommend actions that improve product performance, increase user adoption, and strengthen competitive positioning.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

- Prioritise investment in high-performing application categories.
- Improve underperforming applications through feature enhancements and user experience improvements.
- Benchmark successful applications to identify best practices.
- Maintain regular application updates to improve customer satisfaction and platform compatibility.
- Monitor product KPIs continuously to identify performance changes.

---

## Expected Business Impact

- Improved product quality
- Higher user adoption
- Increased customer satisfaction
- Stronger competitive positioning
