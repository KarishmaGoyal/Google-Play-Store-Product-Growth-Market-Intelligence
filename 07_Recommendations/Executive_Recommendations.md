# 👔 Executive Recommendations

## Objective

Provide strategic guidance to support executive decision-making.

---

## Key Recommendations

### Product Management

- Prioritise investment in high-growth application categories.
- Monitor application performance through executive KPIs.

---

### Customer Experience

- Focus on improving customer ratings and review engagement.
- Use customer feedback to guide product improvements.

---

### Business Strategy

- Evaluate pricing strategies regularly.
- Monitor marketplace competition and category performance.
- Allocate resources based on data-driven performance metrics.

---

### Business Intelligence

- Maintain an interactive Power BI dashboard for continuous performance monitoring.
- Review marketplace KPIs periodically to support strategic planning.

---

## Expected Outcome

Implementing these recommendations will help stakeholders improve product performance, strengthen customer engagement, optimise pricing decisions, and support sustainable marketplace growth through evidence-based decision-making.
