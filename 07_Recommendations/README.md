# 🚀 Business Recommendations

## Overview

This folder presents strategic recommendations derived from the analysis of the Google Play Store dataset. The recommendations are designed to support product growth, improve customer engagement, optimise pricing strategies, and strengthen marketplace competitiveness.

---

## Recommendation Categories

- Product Strategy
- Customer Engagement
- Pricing Strategy
- Marketplace Growth
- Executive Recommendations

---

## Objective

Translate analytical findings into practical business actions that enable stakeholders to make informed, data-driven decisions and achieve sustainable marketplace growth.
