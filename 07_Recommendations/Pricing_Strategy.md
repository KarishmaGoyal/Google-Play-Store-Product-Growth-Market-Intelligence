# 💰 Pricing Strategy

## Objective

Optimise pricing models to maximise user adoption and revenue potential.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

- Evaluate pricing against competing applications.
- Consider freemium models where appropriate.
- Regularly review pricing based on market trends.
- Use promotional pricing to increase user acquisition.
- Analyse category-specific pricing behaviour before introducing premium features.

---

## Expected Business Impact

- Better monetisation
- Increased revenue opportunities
- Improved pricing competitiveness
- Higher conversion rates
