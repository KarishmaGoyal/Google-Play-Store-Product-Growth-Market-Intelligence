# 👔 Executive Insights

## Objective

Provide a high-level summary of the marketplace to support executive decision-making.

---

## Key Business Areas

### Marketplace Performance

Evaluates the overall size and activity of the Google Play Store using key performance indicators such as total applications, installs, ratings, and reviews.

---

### Product Performance

Identifies high-performing applications and categories based on user adoption and customer satisfaction.

---

### Customer Engagement

Assesses user interaction using ratings and review activity to understand customer behaviour.

---

### Pricing Strategy

Compares free and paid applications to evaluate monetisation opportunities.

---

### Marketplace Intelligence

Highlights competitive categories, market trends, and potential growth opportunities.

---

## Executive Value

The insights enable leadership teams to:

- Monitor marketplace performance.
- Prioritise strategic investments.
- Evaluate product success.
- Support long-term product growth.
