# 📈 Business Insights

## Overview

This folder presents the key business insights derived from analysing the Google Play Store dataset. The insights are based on SQL analysis and Power BI dashboards, helping stakeholders understand marketplace trends, product performance, customer engagement, pricing strategies, and growth opportunities.

---

## Insight Categories

The analysis is organised into the following business areas:

- Executive Insights
- Product Insights
- Customer Insights
- Marketplace Insights
- Pricing Insights

---

## Business Objective

The purpose of these insights is to support data-driven decision-making by identifying patterns, opportunities, and strategic actions that improve product performance and marketplace competitiveness.
