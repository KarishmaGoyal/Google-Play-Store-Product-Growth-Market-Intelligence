# Google Play Store Product Growth & Market Intelligence

## Project Overview

Analysed Google Play Store application data to identify the factors driving
user adoption, product performance, monetisation, and marketplace competition.

## Business Problem

Google Play hosts thousands of applications across multiple categories.
Understanding customer behaviour, pricing strategies, and product performance
helps product managers optimise marketplace growth and developer success.

## Objectives

• Evaluate product performance
• Analyse customer behaviour
• Identify monetisation opportunities
• Measure category competitiveness
• Develop executive dashboards
